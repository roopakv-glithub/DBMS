Membership - in

list the student details whose hobby is 'playing

SQL> select * from stu where regno in (select regno from stu_hobby where hobby='playing');

     REGNO NAME             CGPA
---------- ---------- ----------
       102 jai               8.2


Membership - not in

list the student details whose hobby is 'playing'

SQL> select * from stu where regno not in (select regno from stu_hobby where hobby='playing');
